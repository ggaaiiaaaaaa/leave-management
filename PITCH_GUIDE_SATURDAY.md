---

### 2. The Accounting Firm Pivot (Transition to Tier 2 — ₱70k Sweet Spot)
> *"However, standard software doesn't consider how a CPA firm operates during peak engagement periods like March to April. If three senior auditors take unexpected leaves during a major client deadline, audits get delayed.*  
>  
> *That is why we designed **Tier 2: Accounting Practice Suite (₱70,000)**:*
> 1. **2-Tier Approval Workflow**: Senior Audit Lead approves coverage first, then the Managing Partner signs off.
> 2. **Peak Tax Season "Blackout Period"**: The system automatically flags or locks out leaves during critical BIR filing dates unless an authorized partner grants an exemption.
> 3. **DOLE SIL Monetization Engine**: Year-end calculation of unconsumed leaves converted to cash with zero manual formula errors.
> 4. **1-Click Email Approvals**: Partners can approve leaves directly from their phone's email without logging in."*

---

### 3. Closing the Highest Value Deal (Tier 3 — ₱85k Enterprise)
> *"For just an additional ₱15,000 above Tier 2 (**Tier 3 at ₱85,000**), you get the complete Enterprise Package:*
> - **Direct Payroll Sync**: Formatted 1-click export mapped directly to your payroll file so absences and deductions are computed automatically.
> - **Engagement Team Matrix**: Sets minimum staffing thresholds for Audit, Tax, and Bookkeeping teams.
> - **Instant Viber / Teams Alerts**: Instant morning ping when someone calls in sick.
> - **3 Months Full Priority Warranty & Staff Retraining**."*
